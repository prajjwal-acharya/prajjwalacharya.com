  text: Software is not a destination, but a scaffolding for human thought.

whyIBuild:
  - "I build because the world is a series of interconnected systems that are often more fragile than they appear. Construction is an act of stabilization. I find real satisfaction in creating tools that reduce the cognitive load of others, so they can spend their attention on the interesting problem instead of the plumbing underneath it."
  - "The motivation isn't strictly technical — it's closer to aesthetic. There's a specific kind of satisfaction in a well-factored piece of code or a UI that clicks into place, the same kind you get from something well-built in any discipline. That's the thing I'm chasing."
pillars:
  - title: Negative Space
    body: Good engineering is as much about what's left out as what's added. I'd rather remove complexity than ship a feature that adds it.
  - title: First Principles
    body: Break a problem down until the pieces are undeniable truths, then build on the properties of the stack — not on assumptions borrowed from the last project.
  - title: Systems Thinking
    body: No feature is an island. Every change gets evaluated for its downstream effect on the whole system's stability and performance, not just the file it touched.
learningPrinciples:
  - "Depth over breadth. Better to understand the inner workings of one engine than to know the ignition sequence of ten."
  - "The friction method. If it feels too easy, the limits of the system haven't been found yet — go looking for the bottleneck."
  - "Public failure. The fastest way to calibrate a mental model is to ship something and watch how it breaks in the real world."
