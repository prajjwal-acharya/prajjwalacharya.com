## Why it exists

Every developer who builds a custom site on Next.js, Astro, Remix, or similar ends up rebuilding the same content infrastructure: auth, an admin dashboard, a rich text editor, drafts, media uploads, SEO metadata. ContentEngine exists to remove that repetition, without asking anyone to give up their own frontend for a full CMS.

## What it aims to do

Plug into an existing site rather than replace it. Authentication, storage, media, and the editor would each be a swappable adapter, Postgres or Supabase, R2 or S3, Better Auth or Clerk, rather than a fixed backend the whole site has to be built around.

## Design principles

- Developer first, not built for marketers or ecommerce.
- Composable: auth, storage, media, editor, and renderer are each replaceable pieces, not one bundled system.
- Framework, backend, storage, and auth agnostic by design.
